# Super Leader panel ad concepts

The panel has one compact promotion after the discussion. It sits at the bottom of an empty panel and follows the messages when a room is busy. The button opens the Fandom Room community in a new tab.

| Creative | Headline | Supporting copy | Action | Visual direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Cinematic** (installed) | Become a Super Fan 👑 and: | Review all anime; create your own polls; create your own rankings. | Start creating → | Dark orange text plane fading into the supplied anime collage, lighter orange copy, three checked benefits, and a full-width green button. |
| **2 · Pop** | Your take. Their vote. | Review anime. Create polls. Rank the favourites. | Start a poll | Lavender banner with the supplied Goku artwork in a circular crop. |
| **3 · Poster** | Watch it. Rate it. Rank it. | Review anime. Create polls. Rank the best. | Build a ranking | Black grid, pink and purple circle, and a large crop of the Goku artwork. |

The installed creative uses the collage supplied for this project and the dark orange palette chosen for the final banner. The alternatives emphasise polls and rankings. Each has one action and copy that remains readable in a narrow Chrome side panel. This follows [Google's guidance on clear headlines, concise copy, and a relevant CTA](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9823397?hl=en).

The three image previews are [`04-cinematic.png`](images/ad-creatives/04-cinematic.png), [`05-pop.png`](images/ad-creatives/05-pop.png), and [`06-poster.png`](images/ad-creatives/06-poster.png). The layout source is [`preview-image-led.html`](images/ad-creatives/preview-image-led.html).
