Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This notice explains what information FandomRoom handles when you use our website and browser extension, and the choices available to you.

Information we handle How we use it Who receives it Storage and retention Your choices

Information we handle

  • Account information. If you create an account, Supabase Auth processes the email address and account identifier needed to sign you in. If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process the information needed to authenticate you; this can include your email address and basic profile details supplied by Google.
  • Profile and community content. We store your FandomRoom username and the content you choose to submit, such as polls, rankings, images, comments, votes, anime discussion posts, likes, and follows. Your username, threads, images, and comments are visible to other visitors. Individual poll and ranking responses are associated with your account and are not displayed as your personal selections; results are shown in aggregate according to the feature.
  • Website preferences and session data. The website uses browser local storage for your sign-in session and display preference. Supabase and Google may also use cookies or similar technologies as part of authentication and service security.
  • Extension page scan. When you click the FandomRoom extension button on a supported viewing page, the extension reads that page’s URL, title, visible text, and headings to identify the anime or episode. The scan is held in Chrome’s local extension storage so the discussion panel can use it. The extension does not send the scanned page contents to FandomRoom. If you then create a discussion post, the anime or episode context and the post you submit are sent to Supabase.
  • Technical information. Our service providers may process basic technical information, such as device, browser, IP address, and request data, to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure sign-in, show profiles and public discussions, store poll and ranking content and images, record responses, keep your session and preferences, and maintain and troubleshoot FandomRoom.

Who receives information

FandomRoom uses Supabase for authentication, database, and image storage; Google when you choose Google sign-in; and Vercel to host the website. These providers process information to provide their services to us. Public profile names, threads, uploaded images, and comments can be viewed by anyone. If you share a thread to a social platform, that platform handles the link and any information you choose to share under its own privacy terms.

We do not currently use advertising or analytics services on the website, and we do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Storage and retention

Account and community information is stored with Supabase. Browser session and display preferences remain in local storage until they expire, are replaced, or you clear your browser data. Extension scan details remain in Chrome’s local extension storage until they are replaced or you remove the extension or its stored data.

We keep account and community information while it is needed to provide FandomRoom. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account information or content, contact us using the address below. We may retain information where needed for legal, safety, or security reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to create an account, use Google sign-in, post content, or use the browser extension. You can clear local browser data or remove the extension to delete locally stored preferences and scan data. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete, or receive certain personal information. Contact us and we’ll respond to your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice as FandomRoom changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact
